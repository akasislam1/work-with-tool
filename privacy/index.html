Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information when you use Work With Tools. Questions? Contact the site owner.

Your files

The tools run inside your own browser. The images and PDFs you choose are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

Visit statistics

We count visits so we know which tools people use. We record the page, your country, whether you use a phone or a computer, and the website you came from. We do not use cookies for this and we do not store your IP address. To count unique visitors we use a one-way code made from your IP address and browser details together with the date and a secret; it changes every day and cannot be turned back into your address. If your browser sends “Do Not Track”, we do not count your visit.

Free-plan limits

To apply the daily limits of the free plan we keep a counter of how many files were added to each tool today. It is tied to the same kind of one-way code and is deleted after about two days. A similar counter is kept in your browser’s storage.

Accounts (optional)

You only need an account to buy Premium. We store your email address and your password in scrambled (hashed) form; we cannot read your password. We use one cookie to keep you signed in. You can sign out at any time. To have your account deleted, contact us.

Payments

Premium payments are checked by hand. When you ask for Premium you upload a screenshot of your payment and may add a reference. We use it only to check the payment and delete the screenshot as soon as we have decided. We keep a short record of the request (plan, amount, payment method, date and result). We do not receive or store your card number.

Emails

We send an email to tell you whether your payment was approved. We do not send marketing emails.

Advertising

Free visitors may see ads. Advertising companies can use cookies or similar technology to show and measure ads; their own privacy policies apply. Premium members do not see ads.

Your choices

You can block cookies in your browser, turn on “Do Not Track”, or ask us to see or delete the information we hold about you.

Changes

If we change how we handle information we will update this page.
